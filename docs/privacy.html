Leafly Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 10, 2026 · Applies to "Leafly - Ambient Weather & Nature Effects" (the "Extension"), published by Innoartive Labs.

Summary

Leafly does not require an account, does not include analytics or advertising code, and does not send your page content or browsing history to us or anyone else. Your settings stay on your device. The only data that leaves your device is the location information needed for weather and city search, which goes to Open-Meteo.

Data stored on your device

Leafly saves the following in your browser's local extension storage (chrome.storage.local): your preferences (effect, intensity, speed, wind, opacity and similar), your list of excluded websites, your selected location, and the most recent cached weather. This data is not synced to any account and is not sent to the developer. Removing the Extension deletes it. "Reset to Defaults" restores settings but does not delete the saved location or weather cache.

Data sent to a third party: Open-Meteo

When you use city search, the text you type is sent to Open-Meteo's geocoding service. When Weather Sync is on, or when you refresh weather or select a location, the coordinates of your selected location are sent to Open-Meteo's forecast service. Like any web request, this also discloses your IP address and standard connection information to Open-Meteo. In Manual mode, with no search or refresh, Leafly makes no weather requests. Open-Meteo's handling of data is described in its terms and privacy information. Weather data is provided by Open-Meteo under CC BY 4.0 and adapted to Leafly effects.

Website access and activity

Leafly runs on the web pages you visit in order to draw its visual overlay and to detect whether you are idle (to settle and sweep particles). It checks the current site's hostname against your exclusion list. This processing happens entirely on your device. Leafly does not read, collect, store or transmit page content, form data, keystrokes, mouse activity or your browsing history.

Permissions

  • storage - saves your preferences, exclusions, location and cached weather locally.
  • alarms - schedules periodic weather checks when Weather Sync is on.
  • tabs - identifies the current site so you can exclude it, and delivers settings changes and the sweep action to open tabs.
  • Access to all sites (content script) - needed to draw the overlay and detect inactivity.
  • api.open-meteo.com and geocoding-api.open-meteo.com - weather and city search requests.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell or transfer user data to third parties, other than the Open-Meteo requests described above.
  • We do not use or transfer data for purposes unrelated to the Extension's single purpose of displaying ambient effects.
  • We do not use or transfer data to determine creditworthiness or for lending.
  • We do not use analytics, advertising or tracking code.

Children

Leafly is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone.
