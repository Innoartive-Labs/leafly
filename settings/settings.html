Privacy & Performance

How Leafly handles data and manages animation performance.

Data stored on this device

Preferences, excluded domains, the selected location and cached weather are stored in Chrome's local extension storage. Leafly does not use an account or Chrome sync. Removing the extension deletes its local extension data; resetting preferences restores settings but does not delete the location or weather cache.

Weather and location requests

City searches send the search text to Open-Meteo. Weather requests send the selected coordinates to Open-Meteo, including in automatic mode and when you refresh or select a location. The provider also receives normal connection information such as your IP address. Weather data is provided by Open-Meteo and adapted to Leafly effects (CC BY 4.0). See Open-Meteo's terms for its practices. The default location is London.

Website access

Leafly runs on accessible websites to draw effects and detect inactivity. It checks the current hostname against your exclusions. Leafly does not send page content, browsing history, or activity events to its developer, and includes no analytics or advertising code. Browser-restricted pages cannot display effects.

Performance and controls

Effects use canvas animation and a particle pool; browsers may throttle animation in hidden tabs. Lower intensity or disable Leafly to reduce resource use. Reduced motion can be respected in General settings, and individual sites can be excluded.
